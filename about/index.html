A little about me

Making things by hand, and sharing a little joy.

I have always enjoyed making things by hand. Whether it’s a card for a special occasion, a decoration for the home, or a new idea I’m excited to try, creating lets me turn simple materials into something personal. Herzens Werke is where I can share some of what I make and the joy I find in making it.

Every piece starts with care and a bit of imagination. I hope you find something here that inspires you. If you have an idea of your own, I’d love to hear about it.
